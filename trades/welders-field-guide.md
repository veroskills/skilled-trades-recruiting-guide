## Understanding Welding Processes, Positions & Materials

A production shop looking for a high-speed MIG fabricator on thin-gauge carbon steel needs a very different background than a mechanical contractor looking for a 6G TIG/Stick pipe welder on stainless steel. Gathering these specifics upfront in a preliminary conversation gives the staffing recruiter everything they need before calling the candidate.

---

## Core Welding Processes

| Process Name | AWS Designation | Typical Applications & Materials |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MIG (Gas Metal Arc Welding)** | `GMAW` | Production manufacturing, structural fabrication, automotive frames, carbon steel, aluminum spool gun |
| **TIG (Gas Tungsten Arc Welding)** | `GTAW` | Precision fabrication, sanitary stainless tubing, aerospace, thin-wall pipe, exotic alloys, aluminum |
| **Stick (Shielded Metal Arc Welding)** | `SMAW` | Field structural steel erection, outdoor pipeline/pipe work, heavy equipment repair, maintenance |
| **Flux-Cored Arc Welding** | `FCAW` (`Dual Shield` / `Self-Shielded`) | Heavy plate fabrication, structural steel, shipyard/marine, high-deposition shop and field welding |

---

## Standard Welding Positions (`Plate` vs. `Pipe`)

- **Groove Welds on Plate**:
  - `1G` (Flat), `2G` (Horizontal), `3G` (Vertical — uphill or downhill), `4G` (Overhead)
- **Groove Welds on Pipe**:
  - `1G` (Rolled horizontal pipe), `2G` (Fixed vertical pipe), `5G` (Fixed horizontal pipe), `6G` (Fixed pipe at a 45° incline)
- **Fillet Welds**:
  - `1F` (Flat), `2F` (Horizontal), `3F` (Vertical), `4F` (Overhead)

## Practical Preliminary Interview Questions for Welders

During a [VeroSkills Preliminary AI Interview](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-interviews), welders can describe their shop and field experience directly:
- *Which welding processes (`MIG`, `TIG`, `Stick`, `Flux-Core`) do you use most often, and on what materials and thicknesses?*
- *Do you primarily work on structural steel, custom layout/fitting from blueprints, production fixtures, or pipe?*
- *Which weld positions (`2G`, `3G`, `4G`, `6G`) have you performed on recent jobs?*
- *Are you looking for a shop fabrication role, field construction work, or travel turnaround assignments?*
