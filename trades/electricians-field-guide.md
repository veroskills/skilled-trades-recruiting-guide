## Understanding Electrical Trade Specialties

"Electrician" covers vastly different day-to-day work depending on whether the job order is for a residential housing tract, a commercial hospital buildout, or a heavy industrial manufacturing plant. Documenting a candidate's exact electrical environment during the preliminary interview saves recruiters and hiring managers hours of back-and-forth.

---

## Electrical Environments & Core Tasks

| Electrical Specialty | Typical Systems & Voltage | Common Daily Tasks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial Electrician** | 120V–480V 3-phase distribution, lighting, branch circuits, fire alarm, data/low-voltage raceways | Bending EMT, rigid, and IMC conduit (hand benders, hydraulic/electric benders); pulling wire; panel terminations; reading commercial electrical drawings |
| **Industrial Electrician** | 480V+ 3-phase switchgear, motor control centers (MCCs), VFDs, PLCs, instrumentation, cable tray | Troubleshooting 3-phase motors, wiring variable frequency drives and control panels, installing rigid conduit and cable tray, reading ladder logic and schematics |
| **Residential Electrician** | 120V/240V single-phase service panels, Romex (NM-B), lighting fixtures, receptacles, EV chargers | Rough-in wiring, service panel upgrades, trim-out, troubleshooting household circuits |

## Practical Preliminary Interview Questions for Electricians

In a [VeroSkills Preliminary AI Interview](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-interviews), candidates can talk through their hands-on experience in their own words:
- *What mix of commercial, industrial, or residential electrical projects have you worked on over the last few years?*
- *What types and sizes of conduit have you bent and installed (EMT, rigid, PVC, cable tray), and which benders have you used?*
- *Have you worked with 480V 3-phase systems, switchgear, transformers, motor controls, or PLCs?*
- *Tell us about a recent project where you worked from electrical blueprints or schematics.*

All responses are summarized and recorded for the [human recruiter handoff](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/recruiter-handoff).
