## Overview of Manufacturing Staffing

Manufacturing and industrial staffing teams support continuous production lines, precision job shops, automotive suppliers, food and beverage facilities, and aerospace plants. High-volume shift coverage (`1st`, `2nd`, `3rd`, and `2-2-3` rotating continental shifts) and specialized machine operation make rapid outreach and organized recruiter handoffs essential.

| Category | Representative Roles | Typical Equipment & Systems |

| **Machining & Tooling** | CNC Machinists (Mill / Lathe), Manual Machinists, Tool & Die Makers, CNC Setup Operators | Haas, Mazak, Okuma, Fanuc controls, G-code/M-code, micrometers, calipers, GD&T blueprints |

| **Quality & Warehouse Logistics** | Quality Inspectors (CMM), Forklift Operators (Sit-Down / Stand-Up / Reach), Shipping & Receiving | Coordinate Measuring Machines, optical comparators, ERP/WMS scanners, clamp and reach trucks |

---

## Key Preliminary Interview Topics for Manufacturing Candidates

1. **Machine & Control Familiarity**: Whether a machinist performs full setups, tool offsets, and edits or operates pre-set production runs; which machine brands and controllers they have used.
2. **Troubleshooting & Maintenance Scope**: For maintenance roles, experience with mechanical teardown vs. electrical troubleshooting, schematics, hydraulics, and conveyor systems.
3. **Precision Measurement**: Familiarity with calipers, micrometers, gauges, and reading technical shop drawings.
4. **Shift Preferences**: Availability for 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 12-hour rotating shifts and overtime schedules.
