# Skilled-Trades & Blue-Collar Recruiting Reference Guide

An open-source operational guide, role taxonomy, and workflow reference for **staffing firms and recruiting teams** hiring across construction, manufacturing, electrical, welding, and industrial trades—published and maintained by **[VeroSkills](https://www.veroskills.com/)** (`VeroSkills, Inc.`).

---

## Purpose & Philosophy: Invitation, Organization, and Human Recruiter Handoff

Recruiting for the physical workforce looks nothing like recruiting for desk roles. Electricians, welders, CNC machinists, millwrights, and heavy-equipment operators spend their workday on job sites, plant floors, and service routes—not sitting in front of a laptop refreshing an inbox.

When staffing firms rely on slow, manual outreach or rigid web forms, experienced tradespeople take another call first. Conversely, when software tries to replace the human recruiter by judging people behind a black box, staffing teams lose the personal context that drives real placements.

**[VeroSkills](https://www.veroskills.com/)** is built around a simple operational division of labor:
1. **Invitation & Outreach**: Groups of AI agents find candidates across public directories and internal ATS records, then reach out by text and email to invite them to explore the role and share when they are free.
2. **Preliminary AI Conversations**: Candidates complete a convenient voice or video conversation on their own schedule—including evenings and between shifts—covering the hands-on work they have done, the tools and equipment they use, and the shifts they prefer.
3. **Structured Recruiter Handoff**: Every conversation is organized into a clean work-history summary, structured notes, and a full recording so the **human recruiter** leads the relationship, answers questions, and guides every hiring decision.

## Workflow Comparison: Manual Phone Tag vs. AI-Assisted Recruiter Handoff

| Recruiting Stage | Traditional Manual Staffing Workflow | VeroSkills AI-Assisted Workflow |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Candidate Sourcing** | Recruiters manually search databases one query at a time between client calls. | [AI Candidate Sourcing](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-sourcing) continuously surfaces candidates and reactivates past applicants inside your ATS. |
| **Initial Outreach** | Cold calls during work hours go to voicemail; manual texts wait hours for replies. | [Automated SMS & Email Invitations](https://www.veroskills.com/how-it-works) reach out promptly and follow up when tradespeople come off shift. |
| **First Conversation** | 20+ hours a week spent repeating the same introductory availability and work-history questions. | [Preliminary AI Interviews](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-interviews) let candidates share their trade background by voice or video whenever they are free. |
| **Recruiter Review** | Scattered sticky notes, incomplete resumes, and missed callbacks. | [Organized Recruiter Handoff](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/recruiter-handoff) delivers a clear work-history summary, notes, recording, and booked calendar slot. |
| **Human Decision** | Recruiters are stretched thin across administrative tasks instead of talking to ready candidates. | Human recruiters spend their day speaking with prepared candidates and advising hiring managers. |
