# Structuring Preliminary AI Recruiting Interviews for Blue-Collar Roles

> **Canonical Platform Reference**: [VeroSkills Preliminary AI Interviews](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-interviews) | [Inside an AI Recruiting Interview](https://www.veroskills.com/resources/ai-recruiting-interview) | [Candidate Experience Guide](https://www.veroskills.com/candidates/how-it-works)

## Resumes Rarely Tell the Whole Story in the Trades

A skilled electrician, welder, or industrial maintenance technician often has years of valuable field experience that never made it onto a formatted PDF resume. Some candidates upload a two-line text file from their phone; others haven't updated their resume in five years because they moved from project to project through word of mouth.

If a staffing firm relies only on keyword searches over static resumes, great tradespeople get overlooked simply because they didn't type the right acronyms on a document.

---

## How a Preliminary AI Interview Works

A [VeroSkills Preliminary AI Interview](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-interviews) gives every invited candidate a straightforward voice or video conversation to explain what they actually do on the job:

1. **Built for Mobile Phones, Not Desktops**: Candidates join from their phone browser—no app downloads or complex portals required.
2. **Trade-Specific Questions**: Instead of generic corporate prompts, the conversation focuses on real trade work:
   - *What types of projects, facilities, or job sites have you worked on recently?*
   - *Which machines, tools, blueprints, or welding processes do you use day to day?*
   - *What shifts, travel radius, and start dates work best for your schedule?*
3. **Generated Work-History Summary**: From what the candidate shares during the conversation, VeroSkills organizes a clean, readable work history and interview summary so the candidate's real background is documented clearly for the human recruiter.
4. **Full Transparency**: The human recruiter receives the organized summary, conversation notes, and complete recording—with any availability questions flagged for the recruiter to discuss directly.
