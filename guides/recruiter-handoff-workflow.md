# The Human Recruiter Handoff Workflow

> **Canonical Platform Reference**: [VeroSkills Recruiter Handoff](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/recruiter-handoff) | [From AI to Human Recruiter](https://www.veroskills.com/resources/from-ai-to-human-recruiter) | [Solutions for Staffing Firms](https://www.veroskills.com/solutions/staffing-firms)

## Why AI Should Prepare the Recruiter—Never Replace Them

In staffing, relationships win placements. Candidates want to know who they are working for, what the job site culture is like, and whether the staffing firm has their back. Client hiring managers rely on recruiters who understand their shop floor and project timelines.

[VeroSkills](https://www.veroskills.com/) is designed so that **AI handles the early administrative legwork** and **human recruiters lead every personal conversation and hiring decision**.

---

## What Lands on the Recruiter's Desk at Handoff

When a candidate completes their preliminary AI interview and books a follow-up slot, the recruiter receives a complete handoff package inside the platform:

1. **Organized Work History**: A structured account of the candidate's recent roles, employers, project types, and hands-on responsibilities based on what they shared.
2. **Concise Interview Summary & Notes**: Key takeaways on equipment familiarity, shift preferences, commute radius, and target start date.
3. **Full Voice/Video Recording & Transcript**: Recruiters can listen to or watch the exact conversation to hear the candidate's own words before calling them.
4. **Calendar Scheduling & Reminders**: Confirmed time slots and automated text/email reminders so both the candidate and the recruiter show up ready to talk.

Instead of spending the first 15 minutes of a call asking basic logistics questions, the recruiter starts the conversation already knowing the candidate's background—and can focus immediately on relationship building, role fit, and client presentation.
