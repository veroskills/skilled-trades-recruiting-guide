# AI Candidate Sourcing & Shift-Friendly Outreach for Skilled Trades

## Why Desk-Job Outreach Fails in the Skilled Trades

In construction, manufacturing, electrical, and welding roles, the people you want to talk to are actively working with their hands during standard 9-to-5 office hours. They are up on a scissor lift, under a hood running a weld bead, or monitoring a CNC machining center on a loud plant floor.

When a staffing recruiter calls at 10:30 AM on a Tuesday:
1. The call goes to voicemail.
2. By the time the tradesperson finishes their shift at 4:30 PM or 11:00 PM, the recruiter has logged off for the day.
3. Three days of phone tag pass before a basic 5-minute conversation about pay, location, and shift availability ever happens.

---

## How AI Sourcing & Invitation Workflows Solve the Timing Gap

With [VeroSkills](https://www.veroskills.com/), groups of AI agents handle early sourcing and outreach so candidates can respond on their own clock:

### 1. Surfacing Active and Past-Applicant Talent
- **ATS Reactivation**: Staffing firms sit on thousands of historical candidate records from prior job orders. AI agents review work histories and role preferences to surface people who may be ready for a new assignment.
- **Public Talent Discovery**: Agents search across public professional profiles and trade directories to identify tradespeople in the target geography.

### 2. Respectful, Invitation-First SMS & Email Outreach
- **Clear Role Details Up Front**: Outreach messages clearly state the trade role, general location, shift structure, and next steps.
- **Candidate Control**: Every message is an **invitation**, never a demand. Candidates can reply when their shift ends, ask questions, schedule a time, or decline with zero friction.
- **24/7 Responsiveness**: When a 2nd-shift machinist replies at 11:15 PM, the AI agent responds immediately and offers a link to complete a preliminary interview or book time on the recruiter's calendar.
